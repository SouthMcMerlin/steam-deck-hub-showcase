Powered by SteamOS

Built on Arch Linux, SteamOS offers an intuitive, console-like gaming experience tailored specifically for portable play, combined with the unrestricted freedom of a full desktop computer.

Instant Suspend & Resume

Press the power button at any point during your game to pause the action and put the device into low-power sleep mode. Tap it again to pick up immediately where you left off.

Desktop Mode

Switch over to the KDE Plasma Desktop with a single press to browse the web, install Linux applications, or manage your files like a traditional PC.

Seamless Cloud Saves

Start a session on your home PC, pick up your Steam Deck on the go, and continue playing right where your save left off with automatic Steam Cloud Sync.

Why It's Incredible

Trackpad Precision

Dual capacitive trackpads allow you to play cursor-heavy games like strategy titles, CRPGs, and mouse-based puzzle games with high accuracy.

Complete Control Mapping

Remap every button, trigger, and gyro control with Valve's Steam Input, or download community controller layouts created by other players.

Open Ecosystem

No paywalls for online play. Install alternative storefronts like Heroic Games Launcher, run retro emulators, or dual-boot alternative OS options freely.
